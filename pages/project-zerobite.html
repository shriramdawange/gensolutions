GS / 001 · SOCIAL IMPACT INITIATIVE

ZeroBite.

Save food. Share hope. A coordination platform concept connecting safe surplus food with communities that need it.

[ THE IDEA ]

Good food should find a way to someone who needs it.

ZeroBite explores how businesses and communities can redirect safe, edible surplus food to people in need through verified nonprofit partners. Donated food is not a revenue stream; beneficiaries are never charged.

[ THE FLOW ]

Donor listing → partner match → pickup coordination → safe handoff → distribution record. The platform is designed as an operational coordination and reporting layer, with food safety procedures treated as a prerequisite rather than an afterthought.

Current focus is discovery, partner interviews, safety criteria and MVP planning. No live operational coverage is claimed.

PROJECT STATUS

Restart / discovery — MVP planning

FOCUS

Food rescue · verified NGO partners · impact records

PLANNED STACK

Flutter · web admin · Supabase

FIRST PRIORITY

Food safety, verified partners and controlled pilot design.
