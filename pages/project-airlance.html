AIRLANCE.

Coordination for medical logistics, where every handoff matters.

[ THE OPPORTUNITY ]

Make critical consignments easier to coordinate and trace.

Moving blood, samples and medicines involves multiple teams, time-sensitive decisions and important custody handoffs. AIRLANCE is a product initiative exploring a clearer operational timeline for medical consignments.

[ PRODUCT DIRECTION ]

Potential capabilities include priority-aware dispatch, live ETA visibility, handoff checkpoints and cold-chain attestations. These are product directions under exploration, not claims of a deployed service.

[ WHAT MUST BE VALIDATED ]

Healthcare workflows, regulatory expectations, operational ownership, safety-critical edge cases and trust requirements need to be understood with practitioners before product scope is committed.
