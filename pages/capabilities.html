CAPABILITIES / WHAT WE DO

From difficult
questions to working systems.

We connect research, engineering and product thinking to move promising ideas towards useful outcomes.

[ 01 — AI & INTELLIGENCE ]

Make information and workflows work smarter.

We explore applied machine learning, retrieval-augmented generation, AI agents and intelligent automation — grounded in real tasks, relevant data and measurable evaluation.

  • RAG and knowledge retrieval systems
  • Agentic workflows with human review checkpoints
  • Applied ML, forecasting and model evaluation
  • AI prototypes, benchmarking and evaluation harnesses
[ 02 — SOFTWARE ENGINEERING ]

Software built to be dependable.

We design and build maintainable applications and services with explicit trade-offs, sound architecture and clear ownership.

  • Web applications and product interfaces
  • Backend services, APIs and integrations
  • Cloud infrastructure and deployment workflows
  • Data pipelines, observability and documentation
[ 03 — RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT ]

Turn uncertainty into evidence.

We formulate hypotheses, design experiments and create prototypes to answer difficult technical questions before committing to large builds.

  • Technical feasibility and architecture research
  • Experiment design, benchmarking and ablation
  • Rapid prototypes and proof-of-concept systems
  • Research reporting and reproducibility
[ 04 — PRODUCT INNOVATION ]

Build for people, not pitch decks.

We shape early products from problem discovery and scope definition to MVP delivery and real-user learning.

  • Product discovery and user journeys
  • MVP strategy and development
  • Iteration informed by feedback
  • Product positioning and technical roadmaps
[ HOW WE ENGAGE ]

Research, build, collaborate.

Some work begins as a research question. Some starts with a partner's operational challenge. Others are original product bets. We choose a delivery model that fits the problem and are clear about scope, status and what success will mean.
